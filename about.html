Why HR Insider Playbooks Exists

I’ve spent my career inside HR — advising leaders, handling exits, managing risk, and watching how decisions are actually made behind closed doors.

Employees are rarely told the full truth. Not because every HR professional is shady — but because companies optimize for protection, speed, and control.

That gap leaves employees unprepared when things shift: layoffs, quiet firing, delayed final pay, sudden performance pressure, or confusing exits.

These guides are for employees

HR Insider Playbooks are designed to help you understand what’s happening, why it’s happening, and how to respond without giving up leverage.

  • What HR is thinking (even when they won’t say it)
  • What’s standard vs. what’s strategic
  • Where employees accidentally lose power
  • How to document, communicate, and protect yourself

What this is — and what it isn’t

This is not anti-HR. It’s pro-clarity.
