Fired

What this usually means

  • The company decided to end your employment immediately or with a short exit window.
  • Access may be cut fast, even if they say it’s “procedural.”

What companies often do next

  • Send a termination letter and final pay details later.
  • Limit communication to HR or a generic inbox.

What you should do first

  • Check final pay timing for your state and mark the due date.
  • Save offer letters, pay stubs, and any recent performance docs.
