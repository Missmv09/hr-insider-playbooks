Forced quit

What this usually means

  • “Forced quit” means you were pushed to resign even if you didn’t want to.
  • Pressure can look like threats, sudden deadlines, or “resign to keep your record clean.”

What companies often do next

  • Frame it as a voluntary exit to limit unemployment or severance.
  • Ask you to sign paperwork fast and move on.

What you should do first

  • Document changes, pressure, or threats with dates and names.
  • Save any messages or meeting notes that show the push to resign.
