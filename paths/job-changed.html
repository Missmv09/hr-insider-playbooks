Job changed

What this usually means

  • Your role was stripped, duties were moved, or your title no longer matches your work.
  • This can be a quiet reset before a performance narrative.

What companies often do next

  • Say it’s “business needs” while changing expectations overnight.
  • Update goals without giving a clear plan or timeline.

What you should do first

  • Document the before-and-after duties and any title changes.
  • Save org charts, emails, and meeting notes that show the shift.
